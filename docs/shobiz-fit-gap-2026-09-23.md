# Shobiz Havas Fit-Gap Notes

These notes interpret the supplied Shobiz proposal and walkthrough brief as product requirements. They are not instructions to claim unfinished capabilities as shipped.

## Ready to Demonstrate

- Multi-step event definition with days, dates, functions, venues, guest counts, and selected needs.
- Visual event map for moving from event to day, function, and scoped editor.
- Food, design, logistics, task, note, venue, vendor, and service planning.
- Vendor catalogue and service selection tied to event requirements.
- Run-of-show, contextual booking messages, budget, and event-readiness logic.
- Client, vendor, manager, and admin role boundaries.
- Management event and booking registers.
- Admin-controlled final pricing and full client-price billing records.
- Separate client collection and vendor settlement directions.

## Foundation Exists, Shobiz Configuration Is Needed

- Management visibility exists, but Shobiz stages, blockers, and portfolio metrics are not configured.
- Current roles are client, vendor, manager, and admin. They are not yet Shobiz departments such as commercial, production, creative, hospitality, and logistics.
- Tasks and readiness exist, but department ownership, dependencies, approval gates, escalations, and change controls need Shobiz rules.
- Messages and notifications exist, but Shobiz channels and escalation expectations need mapping.
- Event pricing and billing exist, but they do not generate a Shobiz quotation from a client brief.

## Proposed, Not Yet Built

- Ingesting a client email, RFP, brief, and attachments.
- Extracting scope and missing information into a review queue.
- Shobiz rate cards, mark-ups, commercial exceptions, and quotation templates.
- Multi-level quotation approval and exception routing.
- Preparing a client email and attachment for human-approved dispatch.
- Department-specific work queues and portfolio dashboards.
- Integration with Shobiz email, files, finance, CRM, or project systems.
- Pilot analytics for handoffs, turnaround time, follow-ups, and ownership clarity.

## Recommended Pilot Order

1. Map one anonymized brief and the quotation it produced.
2. Model the required fields, service categories, rate rules, and exception cases.
3. Map preparer, checker, approver, and final sender responsibilities.
4. Configure one sample event and its participating departments.
5. Test the existing coordination foundation with named evaluators.
6. Build only the smallest quotation and approval slice needed for the pilot.
7. Compare measured before-and-after workflow results before expanding scope.

## Decisions Needed From Rayyan and Shobiz

1. Can Shobiz provide one anonymized brief, its final quotation, and the approval trail used to produce it?
2. Which departments and named roles must participate in the first pilot, and which information must each role be allowed to edit, approve, or only view?
3. For the first quotation pilot, should the system stop at a review-ready draft, or also prepare an email and attachment for a human to approve and send?
