## Honest Boundaries

- Guest communication exports are human-approved; no WhatsApp/email provider send is simulated.
- Online client payment activation remains disabled until provider/KYC/webhook approval.
- Private identity documents and full PNRs are intentionally excluded from broad workspaces and exports.
- A dedicated inventory quantity/issue/return reconciliation board is not yet a first-class screen; the production dossier currently carries those records.
- The dataset is a workflow reconstruction, not a claim that every confidential source row was imported.
